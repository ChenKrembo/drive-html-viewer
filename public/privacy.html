Privacy Policy

HTML Viewer for Drive. Last updated 27 September 2026.

Summary

HTML Viewer for Drive runs entirely in your browser. It has no server of its own and the developer receives none of your data.

Data the app accesses

  • Files you open with the app. When you choose "Open with" in Google Drive, your browser downloads that file directly from Google and displays it. The file is not sent anywhere else by the app.
  • File metadata. The name, type, size and Drive link of the opened file, used to show the title and the "Open in Drive" link.
  • Your Google account ID. Google Drive passes it to the app so that sign-in targets the account that owns the file.

Google permissions

  • drive.file: access only to files you open with this app.
  • drive.install: adds the app to the "Open with" menu in Google Drive.

The app cannot list, read or change any other file in your Drive.

Storage

A short-lived Google access token is kept in your browser's session storage and is discarded when the tab closes. The app uses no cookies, analytics or advertising.

Sharing

The app does not collect, store, sell or share your data. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Content of viewed files

A file you open is displayed in an isolated frame. Scripts inside that file can make their own network requests, as they would if you opened the file locally. Only open files you trust.

Third parties

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log requests such as IP addresses according to GitHub's own privacy statement. Sign-in is handled by Google.

Removing access

In Google Drive, open Settings, then Manage apps, and disconnect HTML Viewer. You can also revoke access from your Google Account under third-party connections.

Contact

Questions and requests: github.com/ChenKrembo/drive-html-viewer/issues.
